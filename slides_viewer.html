Making Real-Time Airfare Inflation Tracking Transparent for National CPI

SIH 2025
Proposed Solution / Approach

• Assured Data Ingestion (AirScrape Engine): Automated daily scraping across 20 high-density DGCA domestic trunk corridors covering 6 airlines and major OTAs to replace slow quarterly physical ticket counter surveys.

• Yield Curve Economics: Our dynamic yield engine moves beyond simple spot prices. It captures 5 advance booking horizons (T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30, T+45) to reflect true consumer booking behavior and surge premiums.

• Official DGCA Weighting: Automatically normalizes route weights based on 109 Million annual domestic passengers across 20 city-pair corridors for statistically rigorous CPI representation.

• Superlative Fisher Index: Calculates the geometric mean of Laspeyres and Paasche indices, eliminating consumer substitution bias during festive travel spikes and holiday price surges.

• Automated TMU Backtesting: Continuously validates computed route fares against official DGCA Tariff Monitoring Unit domestic fare benchmarks (achieving Pearson r = 0.6639).

❓ Potential Challenges and Risks
01
Anti-Scraping / Bot Detection: Airline portals employ Cloudflare / Akamai bot blockers.
02
Dynamic Pricing Outliers: Midnight flash sales or last-minute extreme fares distort index averages.
03
Traffic Weight Network Drift: Airline route expansions alter corridor passenger distribution.
04
Missing / Sold-Out Flights: High load-factor flights sell out, creating price voids.
05
Legacy System Latency: Harmonizing modern high-frequency feeds with monthly MoSPI cycles.
🛡️ Strategies For Overcoming Challenges
01
Playwright Stealth + Proxy Rotation: Rotate residential IPs and integrate official OTA APIs.
02
Vectorized Grouped IQR Cleaning: Flag statistical anomalies per corridor+horizon group.
03
Annual DGCA Recalibration: Dynamic weight rebalancing from official annual DGCA statistics.
04
Econometric Imputation: Fill sold-out slots using carrier mean fare spreads and regression curves.
05
Dual-Cadence OpenAPI Endpoints: Provide daily real-time nowcasts and monthly consolidated tables.
