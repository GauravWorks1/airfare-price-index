Official CPI Econometric Specifications

Modified Laspeyres Price Index

Standard official CPI formulation maintaining constant consumption weights from the base period:

I_L^t = [ Σ (w_r * p_r_t) / Σ (w_r * p_r_0) ] × 100

Where w_r is the DGCA passenger volume weight for corridor r.

Superlative Fisher Ideal Index

Geometric mean of Laspeyres and Paasche price indices that satisfies time reversal tests:

I_F^t = √( I_L^t × I_P^t )

Eliminates upward substitution bias during festival fare surges.
